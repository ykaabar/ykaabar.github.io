A measuring equipment fleet always ready for audit

Every inspection, measuring and test equipment has its record, its status and its next due date. Overdue items show up before the auditor finds them.

The problem

Before: an Excel file that did not tell the whole story

Before

  1. The whole fleet in a single Excel file
  2. Equipment that no longer existed still listed as active
  3. Calibration delays that were not real
18pieces of equipment wrongly active in the old file

Now

  1. Every item has a clear status: monitored, not monitored, retired
  2. Due dates and delays recomputed every day
  3. Planning of internal checks and external shipments
  4. Register exportable to show as is
Up-to-date fleetand ready to show in an audit
On the shop floor

Rolling out the tool revealed 18 pieces of equipment still active although they no longer existed. In the old file, they showed as overdue for calibration: the application brought the gap to light before an auditor did.

The application

The fleet, the due dates, the decisions

Register and due dates

The state of the fleet on one screen.

  • Record per item: location, interval, internal or external calibration
  • Overdue checks and 30-day due dates
  • Fleet compliance rate
  • Unmonitored and retired items kept apart from active ones
  • Import from Excel, register export

Checks and planning

From entering the result to the decision.

  • Check entry and computation of the next due date
  • NOK results: equipment blocked, decision required
  • 3-month planning
  • Shipments grouped by external laboratory
  • Gage R&R study launched from the record
