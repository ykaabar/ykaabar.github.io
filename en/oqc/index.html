Shipping · OQC

The right sample, the right verdict, for every lot

Before shipping, the app gives the sample size and acceptance criteria according to ISO 2859-1, records the inspection and keeps the rejection rate up to date.

The application

From inspected lot to handled non-conformity

Lot inspection

Data entry follows the inspection itself.

  • ISO 2859-1 sampling plan: code letter, sample size, Ac and Re
  • Inspection level and AQL per customer
  • OK or NOK verdict computed from the defects entered
  • Series and prototype phases tracked separately
  • Full history, register export

Follow-up and non-conformities

What comes out of the inspection is followed through.

  • Rejection rate by month, phase and customer
  • Target displayed with the gap to target
  • Non-conformity record for every rejected lot
  • Printable non-conformity report
  • Latest rejected lots always visible
